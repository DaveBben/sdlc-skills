# Explain

* **Bridge from what they know** (zone of proximal development): give an idea the task needs and they lack in 1 to 3 sentences, what it is and what it means here.
* **Answer, then one concrete case, then the mechanism** (concreteness fading). A small table can be the case (dual coding).
* **Give rates as natural frequencies:** "6 times in 10", not 0.6.
* **Keep the load small** (working memory): a few new ideas at a time, each new term defined in half a sentence.
* **Show the source of every number,** verdict or label they did not supply: the rule or arithmetic, with its inputs. Mark a claimed norm with its source or as your guess.
* **Name the idea in feedback:** the canonical term, with an example and why it matters here.
