# Triage a backlog of bugs

Read the open bugs by the person's label or query, else `bug`.

* **Per bug, in order:** a duplicate, searched first; reproducible, run only where the decision turns on it; severity from the harm; a decision from the report's list.
* **Live harm** skips the queue: contain it first.
* **Keep `unconfirmed` apart** from confirmed.
* **One table,** `| Bug | Severity | Decision | Why |`, which the person edits before anything is applied. Ask only about a `won't fix`, or a `story` that needs a product decision.
* **Escape** once per group of bugs in one file or of one kind.
