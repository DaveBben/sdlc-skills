# Triage a backlog of bugs

Read the open bugs by the person's label or query, else `bug`. The person is the one owner who decides; you prepare every row.

* **Per bug, in this order:** a duplicate (search before anything else); reproducible, running it only where the decision turns on it; severity from the harm; a decision from the report's list.
* **Live harm** skips the queue: contain it first, by SKILL.md.
* **Keep `unconfirmed` apart** from confirmed, so the open count means something.
* **One table,** `| Bug | Severity | Decision | Why |`, and the person changes any row before anything is applied. Ask only about a `won't fix`, or a `story` that needs a product decision.
* **Escape** once per group of bugs in one file or of one kind, not per bug.
